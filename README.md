## About

I am a South African developer with a background in **Geography and Geosciences**, now working across **software development, applied AI, data engineering and business systems architecture**.

I build software that replaces disconnected processes with clear workflows, structured data and interfaces people actually want to use. My approach combines research, product thinking, database design, application development and automation, from understanding the operational problem through to shipping the system that solves it.

> **Focus:** making complex operations easier to understand, manage and improve through software.
