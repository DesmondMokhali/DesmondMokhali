### About me

I'm a South African developer with a background in **Geography and Geosciences**, now working across **software development, applied AI, data engineering and business systems architecture**.

I build digital solutions that replace disconnected processes with clearer workflows, structured data and useful interfaces. My approach brings together research, product thinking, database design, application development and automation — from understanding the operational problem to implementing the system.

> **My focus:** Making complex operations easier to understand, manage and improve through software.
